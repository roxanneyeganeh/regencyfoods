Feature · The pecanp. 12

The pecan, properly roasted.

Mild roasting, a buttery texture and a rich flavour. Why our bestseller earns its place in the pastry section and the snack bowl.

Our premium pecans are mildly roasted, which keeps their buttery texture and brings out a rich, rounded flavour without bitterness. They are naturally high in healthy fats, and rich in vitamin E and essential minerals like manganese and copper.

In the kitchen they work twice: chopped into pies, brownies and granola, or served whole as a nutritious snack. They support heart and metabolic health and may help reduce inflammation, which makes them an easy addition to a menu.

We pack them in our own 1kg bags, sealed for freshness, and deliver them next day from our North London hub with the rest of your order.

Perfect for baking, or as a nutritious snack.
