Our story

Thirty years on, the same promise.

Regency Foods started in 1995 with a simple goal: to deliver high-quality ingredients, reliably and quickly, to professional kitchens.

Founded by a former restaurateur, we understand what chefs need: not just good products, but consistent service and support behind the scenes.

Today, we supply restaurants, hotels, pubs and caterers across the UK, with next-day delivery from our North London hub.

We've grown with the industry, expanding our product range and embracing the rise of fusion cuisine. Our focus is on flavour, flexibility and fast turnaround.

Now that same promise comes home: a bold, generous range for the pantry as well as the pass.

Vision

To lead the UK wholesale food sector in delivering diverse, high-quality ingredients, with a specialty in fusion food, that inspire innovation and elevate culinary standards.

Mission

To provide chefs and food businesses with fast, reliable access to premium ingredients and tailored support, blending tradition and modernity in every product we deliver.

How we work

Generous, dependable and quietly playful. Professional in what we promise; joyful in how we show it. A trade supplier with the warmth of a family kitchen.
